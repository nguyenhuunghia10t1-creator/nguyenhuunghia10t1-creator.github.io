Engineering approach

Define. Evaluate.
Integrate. Verify.

Resolve the difficult assumptions early. Agree on evidence before calling a system ready.

01 / Define

Understand the process

Document the operating conditions, sample parts, interfaces and measurable acceptance criteria.

02 / Evaluate

Prove the difficult part

Test acquisition, algorithms and timing on representative inputs before expanding scope.

03 / Integrate

Connect the complete system

Align software contracts, machine states and fault handling across sensors, compute and control.

04 / Verify

Make handover repeatable

Validate against agreed criteria and prepare configuration, operating guidance and maintenance records.
