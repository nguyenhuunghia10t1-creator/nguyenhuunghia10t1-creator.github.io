Embedded Systems

Integrate sensing, firmware and communication within practical electrical and timing constraints.

The industrial problem

Embedded integration spans voltage levels, power quality, sensor behavior and firmware timing. A working bench prototype may fail under noise, reboot or prolonged operation.

The solution approach

Specify the electrical interfaces and timing budget. Structure firmware around defined states, watchdogs and fault reporting. Separate sensing and communications from actuator enable conditions.

Reference architecture

01 →Sensors + electrical interface
02 →MCU / firmware state machine
03 →Signal conditioning + sampling
04 →Communication interface
05 →Supervisory controller
06 / OUTPUTDiagnostics + configuration

Illustrative sequence. Interfaces and timing are specified for the actual equipment and process.

Typical applications

  • Sensor nodes and data-acquisition devices
  • Machine-interface modules
  • Embedded gateways
  • Custom controller prototypes
  • Edge-connected instrumentation

Expected business value

Application-specific sensing and interfaces with defined behavior. Hardware suitability, environmental tolerance and lifetime depend on validation.

What needs validation

  • Check power, signal levels and protection requirements.
  • Exercise reboot, timeout and watchdog recovery.
  • Evaluate noise sensitivity and extended-operation behavior.
Prototype firmware and hardware require additional environmental, electrical and compliance assessment before production use.
