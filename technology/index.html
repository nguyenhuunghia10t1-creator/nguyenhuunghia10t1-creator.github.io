Control boundaries

Failure behavior belongs
in the architecture.

Missing images, low-confidence inference, late results and broken connections are operating states to design for. Every interface needs an owner, a timeout and a recovery path.

  • AI supplies a result; the controller validates whether it may be acted on.
  • Emergency stops and protective functions remain in the safety and power-control design.
  • Job identifiers and acknowledgement prevent results from applying to the wrong part.
  • Stale or unavailable data stays visible to the operator.
